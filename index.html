How to play
  • Pass the phone around. Each player privately sees their word, then hides it.
  • Going around the table from the first speaker, everyone gives one short clue about their word.
  • Civilians all share the same word. Undercover players have a similar word and don't know they're undercover. Mr. White has no word and bluffs.
  • Discuss, then vote someone out. The phone reveals who they were.
  • Civilians win when every undercover and Mr. White is out.
  • Undercover and Mr. White win when they equal or outnumber the civilians left.
  • If Mr. White is voted out, they get one guess at the civilians' word. Guess right and Mr. White wins alone.
